Technical Skills

Microbiology & Fermentation

Microbial culture, isolation, and identification (bacteria, fungi); enzyme induction and screening from fermented foods

Separation & Purification

MPLC, FPLC, Preparative HPLC; column chromatography (HP-20, LH-20, C18); enzyme purification; DNA extraction; gel electrophoresis

Enzyme Characterization

Kinetic analysis (Michaelis-Menten, Lineweaver-Burk, Arrhenius, Dixon); inhibition assays (tyrosinase, glucosidase)

Compound & Structural Analysis

HPLC, UPLC, TLC, SDS-PAGE; dietary fiber and sugar composition; FT-IR; molecular docking

Nanoparticle Fabrication

Nanoemulsion and nanoparticle preparation; characterization by DLS, SEM, TEM, FT-IR, and electronic tongue analysis

Bioactivity Assays

Antioxidant activity (DPPH, ABTS, FRAP, ORAC); cell culture and functional assays (adipogenesis, neuroprotection, muscle protection, skin whitening); Western blot, PCR, qRT-PCR, immunocytochemistry; in vivo models (atopic dermatitis, muscle atrophy)

Statistics & Data Analysis

Response surface methodology (RSM), principal component analysis (PCA); SPSS, SAS
